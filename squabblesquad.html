Birds at Arms

A Multiplayer Online Battle Arena game. Play as one of five unique bird classes, each with their own special ability, and help your team's minions destroy the enemy base!

  • C++
  • SDL 2.0
  • Box2D
  • TinyXML
  • Steamworks
  • P2P multiplayer
View code on GitHub Dev blog

About the project

Birds at Arms is a MOBA developed for our undergraduate senior thesis project. It features a built-from-scratch game engine written in C++ and peer-to-peer 8-player multiplayer with Steam integration.

Developed by Ethan Shimooka, Vincent Moudy, Ricardo Murillo, Brandon Jarvinen, Pourya Soubahti, Vince Chen, Blake Williams, and myself. Artwork by Cris Doi and sound composed by Lizzy Asarnow.
